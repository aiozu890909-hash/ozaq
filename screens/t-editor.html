[Paragraph 2] In the spring of 1858, following the infamous "Great Stink" of the River Thames, civil engineering pioneer Joseph Bazalgette presented plans for a unified subterranean sewer interceptor. While contemporary municipal leaders readily recognized the epidemiological crisis of water-borne pathogens, the execution encountered vehement parliamentary delays.
